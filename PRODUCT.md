# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router, TypeScript), chosen by the user over plain HTML and React + Vite. Plain CSS, no UI or animation library. Static export (`output: "export"`) so it can be hosted on Vercel or GitHub Pages; the deploy target is not decided yet.

## Users

People who reach Dillon's link from a résumé, LinkedIn, Instagram, GitHub, or a conversation: internship recruiters and hiring managers, professors and research groups, hackathon teammates, and classmates. They want to learn, in about a minute, who Dillon is, what they build, how far along it is, and how to get in touch.

## Product Purpose

A personal site for Dillon Kao, a second-year Computer Science student at the University of Virginia. It introduces Dillon, presents their projects as ideas with honest progress (what is done, what is next), and gives working ways to reach them. Success: a visitor leaves knowing Dillon's interests (software development, artificial intelligence, embedded systems), has seen real work, and can email or follow them.

## Positioning

Projects are shown as ideas with visible progress rather than as polished case studies or a list of repositories. The range is the point: a native C++ input engine, LLM evaluation-awareness research, a single-cell bioinformatics dashboard, a hackathon Chrome extension, and a data-auditing pipeline for student funding.

## Operating Context

Opened on phones (from Instagram or LinkedIn bios) and on laptops (from résumés and email). One home page plus a detail page per project.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Contact and social links are exactly: Email (dillonkao@gmail.com), GitHub (https://github.com/dcow12), LinkedIn (https://www.linkedin.com/in/dillonkao), Instagram (https://www.instagram.com/kaodillon/). No others. They appear as logo icons at the top of every page.
- English only. No language switcher.
- No newsletter or email-update signup.
- No profile picture yet; the user will add one later.
- No links on projects yet (no repository links); the user will supply links later.
- No project cover images yet; the user asked to wait before any repository images are used.
- No cursor-following glow or other cursor effects.
- One theme only: a plain black background, with no light mode, theme toggle, or background decoration.
- The tagline under the name is exactly "2nd year Computer Science @ UVA".

## Brand Commitments

- At the user's request the site is modeled on elliotlindberg.com: a minimal single column, a serif name with a highlighted surname, quiet text links, a faint grid background, and staggered entrance motion. Animations should feel similar to the reference without copying it.
- Voice: first person, plain, specific, confident without hype.

## Evidence on Hand

- Bio and fun facts from the user's GitHub profile README (also pasted by the user): second-year CS at UVA; enjoys projects spanning software and hardware; interested in software development, AI, and embedded systems; loves piano; AYCE sushi record of 130 pieces; Roblox developer.
- Project READMEs and source on github.com/dcow12: ControllerBridge-Updated, Privy, ObesiMap, Misty, Data-Cleaning-for-DS-Appropriations-R3-Funding.
- Each project's status and "next" items are inferred from those READMEs and need the user's confirmation.
- Absent, and not to be invented: photos, screenshots on the site, metrics, users, awards, employers, testimonials.

## Product Principles

1. Honest progress over polish: every project says what works and what is next.
2. Scannable in a minute: who, what, how far, how to reach out.
3. Ready to grow: links, covers, and a photo slot can be filled in without a redesign.
